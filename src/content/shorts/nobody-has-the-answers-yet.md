A post going around traces this back to something Kent Beck keeps repeating on his podcast
"Still Burning": nobody has the answers. Ask three experienced engineers a question like how
long an agent should work before you clear its context, and you get five different answers,
ask again in two weeks and you get five new ones.

I know that feeling well. Since joining the Symfony AI Core Team, I notice it even more
clearly: not even there does anyone have finished answers. We're building the foundations
live, while we're still arguing about them.

I still see it as an opening. If there's no consensus yet, anyone can help write it instead
of just copying what worked for someone else. That's why I keep trying to actually measure
an open question instead of just discussing it: most recently, whether an agent uses a tool
I build at all, unprompted, MCP against CLI against Skills. A small experiment with 20 runs
taught me more than every opinion I'd read about it before.
