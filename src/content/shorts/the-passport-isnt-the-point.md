A growing argument on my feed: switch to Chinese AI models, because they ship open
weights and open beats closed.

Open weights are a real property. You can download the file, inspect it, run it
yourself. What they don't guarantee is what's actually baked into the answers. Several
open-weight models trained in China deflect or refuse on specific political topics, not
as an API filter you could route around, but trained into the weights themselves.
American models carry their own version of the same thing: different topics, same
mechanism, alignment choices baked in by whoever trained them.

That doesn't make the two sides interchangeable. Western values sit closer to mine, and
probably to most people reading this. China's political system adds another layer on top:
it's simply a more sensitive environment for a model to be shaped in. None of that makes
American alignment some neutral default, just a reason not to treat the comparison as
perfectly even.

I run closed and open models, local and via API, American and Chinese, depending on the
task. That mix doesn't excuse me from checking what I'm actually dealing with. Know the
model, and stay critical of all of it.
